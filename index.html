Taming the Watery Stool: Why It Happens & How to Fix It

Motility Fix

Turning erratic morning watery trickles into smooth, complete, formed bowel movements

💡 Why Emma Gets "Watery Bypass" Instead of a Full Clear:

In slow transit colons, Linaclotide draws large amounts of water into the bowel. If the lower bowel (sigmoid colon) has slow-moving stool, the water cannot hydrate the solid mass quickly enough. Instead, fluid pressure forces the water to channel *around* the stool as an uncomfortable, watery trickle. This is called paradoxical bypass—not true diarrhea!

Step 1: The 40-Min Fast

Take Linaclotide first thing on waking with 350ml water + electrolyte. Strictly wait 40 mins before food so the medicine coats your bowel undisturbed.

Step 2: Warm Trigger

40 mins after waking dose, sip 150ml warm water or peppermint tea right before your Blueberry Form Shake. This naturally fires the gastrocolic reflex!

Step 3: Squatty Angle

Use a footstool so your knees are higher than your hips (35° angle). This unkinks the puborectalis muscle so formed stool drops effortlessly.

Why Your Tummy Pushes Out (And How to Release It)

Super Simple

The 2-minute explanation of your post-meal tummy tightness

Emma, your post-meal tummy tightness is not body fat, not your fault, and not extra food weight. It is simply a temporary muscle mix-up: after eating, your breathing muscle (diaphragm) pushes downward instead of floating up, forcing your tummy outward and making your waistband feel tight.
⚠️ Why It Feels Tight & Hard to Catch a Breath

When your breathing muscle pushes down, your upper chest muscles tense up to compensate. This makes it feel hard to catch a deep breath and presses right under your left ribs. It is 100% physical muscle pressure, not your imagination!

✨ How the 3-Minute Breath Fixes It

When you breathe out slowly for 6 seconds, your breathing muscle floats back up into your chest. Your stomach gets its space back, your ribs unpinch, and your tummy softens and goes flat naturally.
