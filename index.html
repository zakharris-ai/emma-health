The Morning Wake-Up Golden Rule for Linaclotide

Crucial Rule

Emma, your past notes showed doses taken at erratic times (like 3:49 AM, 5:37 AM, or 11 PM). When taken late at night or right after eating, it triggers sudden, exhausting liquid stomach dumps and painful rebound cramps. Always take it first thing on waking, on an empty stomach, with a large glass (400ml) of warm water, and wait 30–45 minutes before having breakfast. This gives your bowel the smooth, predictable morning wake-up call it needs.
