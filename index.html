🧠 Why Your Tummy, Trousers & Head Feel Like This Today
👖
Why your trousers / bottoms feel tight:

This is NOT body fat and you haven't gained weight. Progesterone naturally relaxes your intestinal smooth muscle (so your bowel moves slower) and signals your kidneys to hold 1–2 kg of harmless fluid. It is 100% temporary biology.

🧠
Why your ADHD brain won't stop hyper-focusing on it:

When progesterone rises and dopamine dips, your sensory filter relaxes. A waistband or tummy pressure feels 10x louder to your nervous system than usual. You're not being dramatic—your brain is experiencing real sensory magnification. Permission granted to wear your softest, stretchiest bottoms all day today!

🫁
Why there's trapped air or fullness under your ribs:

Because transit is slower, gas moves sluggishly past the left rib bend (splenic flexure). Doing a quick 3-minute diaphragmatic release helps your belly expand outwards naturally without pushing up into your chest.
